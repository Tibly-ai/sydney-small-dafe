Program Note

Sydney Small has spent the better part of a decade learning what a room wants before it knows it wants it. She started in a basement club on Ludlow Street with a three piece band and a microphone that cut out twice a set, and she has since carried that same unhurried phrasing to rooms that hold twelve people and rooms that hold twelve hundred. Her sets move between Ellington, Nina Simone, and a handful of originals written on the A train, delivered with the kind of restraint that makes a lyric land harder for arriving quietly.

This site exists for two reasons. One, so you can find out where she's singing next and reserve a seat before the room fills. Two, so if you're planning a wedding, a private dinner, a corporate gathering, or a night that needs a voice in the room, you can tell her what you have in mind and what you'd pay for it. She reads every request herself.
