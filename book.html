Private Bookings

Book Sydney Small for your next occasion

Sydney sings an evening built around the room she's given: a quiet corner set of jazz standards for a rehearsal dinner, a full band of torch songs and soul for a milestone birthday, or a single microphone and piano for a corporate reception that needs a little warmth. Every booking starts as a conversation, not a package. Tell us the date, the venue, and what you can put toward it, and Sydney reads through every request herself before her team calls to work out the details.

01

Private Parties

Anniversaries, milestone birthdays, engagement dinners. Sydney tailors the set list to the guests in the room, mixing requests with her own arrangements of standards from Ella to Nina Simone.

02

Corporate & Private Venues

Holiday parties, client dinners, gallery openings. Sydney has performed for firms across Midtown and Tribeca and works easily with in-house AV and event staff.

03

Weddings & Ceremonies

From a first dance to a full cocktail hour set, Sydney has sung at ceremonies and receptions across the five boroughs and the Hudson Valley.
